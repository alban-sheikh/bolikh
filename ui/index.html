Guide

Hold Right ⌥, speak, and let go: your words are typed where your cursor is. Tap the key once to talk hands-free and tap again to finish. Below is everything you can say.

Lines and lists

Say the command in the middle of your sentence, without pausing for it.

You sayBolikh types
Hi Priya new line thanks for the updateHi Priya Thanks for the update
Here is the plan new paragraph first we testHere is the plan First we test

Shopping numbered list milk next item eggs next item breadShopping: 1. Milk 2. Eggs 3. Bread

A numbered list keeps counting while you keep talking. When you start a new dictation, say “numbered list” again and it starts from 1.

Pressing Esc while recording cancels it, and nothing is typed.

Punctuation

Bolikh adds commas, full stops and question marks by itself. Say them when you want to be exact.

You sayBolikh types
Hello comma how are you question markHello, how are you?
That is great exclamation markThat is great!
this is the end full stopthis is the end.
see the notes open bracket page two close bracketsee the notes (page two)
he said open quote hello close quotehe said “hello”
state hyphen of hyphen the hyphen artstate-of-the-art
wait ellipsiswait…

Also: colon, semicolon, open and close square bracket, open and close curly bracket, em dash. “Period” and “dash” are left as words, because people say them in normal sentences; say “full stop” and “hyphen”.

Recording

Stop after a pauseIn hands-free dictation, Bolikh finishes by itself when you stop talking. Holding the key is not affected.

Output

Remove filler wordsStrips "um", "uh", "hmm" from what you say.
Write numbers as digits"forty eight" becomes 48, "fifty rupees" ₹50, "three thirty pm" 3:30 PM, "twenty first" 21st. English only; "one" to "ten" on their own stay as words.
Remove repeated words"the the plan" becomes "the plan".
Spoken symbols and addressesSay "comma", "question mark", "open bracket", "hashtag", "slash" or "john at gmail dot com" and the symbol is typed.
Voice commands"new line", "new paragraph", "bullet point", "numbered list" then "next item", "all caps" before a word, "tab key", and "scratch that" to delete what you just dictated.
Add a space after each dictationSo consecutive dictations flow naturally.
Paste shortcutTerminals paste with Ctrl+Shift+V. Automatic picks it when a terminal is focused; choose it yourself if text doesn't appear in your terminal.
Restore clipboardPut back what you had copied after the text is inserted.
