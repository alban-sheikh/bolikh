Bolikh / Mac

Bolikh for macOS · free

Dictation for Mac that works in every app, offline.

Bolikh is a free dictation app for macOS. Hold the Right Option key, speak, and let go: your words are typed wherever your cursor is, in Mail, Slack, Notes, Word, your browser or a terminal. Speech is turned into text on your Mac, so it works without the internet and your voice is never uploaded.

macOS

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 12 Monterey or later. Intel Macs aren’t supported yet.

Download for Mac

Open the .dmg and drag Bolikh to Applications.

Why use Bolikh on Mac

One key, any app

Hold Right Option to talk and release to type. It works in any app where you can type, and you can change the key or switch to press-once-to-start in Settings.

Runs on your Mac’s own chip

The speech model runs locally on Apple Silicon. After the one-time model download (about 670 MB) you can dictate on a plane or with Wi-Fi off.

Text that reads as written

Bolikh removes “um” and “uh”, adds punctuation, writes numbers as digits and understands commands such as “new line” and “bullet point”.

Lives in the menu bar

No window to manage. Bolikh waits in the menu bar and shows a small indicator while it listens.

Set up Bolikh on Mac

Bolikh needs two macOS permissions: Microphone, to hear you, and Accessibility, so the key works while you are in other apps and Bolikh can paste the text for you.

  1. 1

    Install

    Open the .dmg you downloaded and drag Bolikh into Applications. Then open Bolikh from Applications.

    If macOS says it can’t verify Bolikh, see “macOS won’t open Bolikh” below.

  2. 2

    Allow the microphone

    Click Test in the setup screen and say a few words. macOS asks if Bolikh may use the microphone: click Allow. Bolikh then tells you the microphone works.

  3. 3

    Turn on Accessibility

    Click Open Settings in Bolikh. In Privacy & Security → Accessibility, switch Bolikh on, then come back. The step ticks itself off.

    This lets the key work while you’re in other apps, and lets Bolikh paste for you.

  4. 4

    Let the speech model download

    Bolikh downloads its speech model once (about 670 MB) and shows the progress. After that it works offline.

  5. 5

    Try it

    Click in the practice box, hold Right Option, say a sentence and let go. Bolikh then waits in the menu bar. Open Settings from there to change the key or how it behaves.

Problems and fixes on Mac

Does Bolikh work on my Mac?

Open the Apple menu → About This Mac. If it says Chip: Apple M1 (or M2, M3, M4 and later), Bolikh works. If it says Processor: Intel, it doesn’t yet: the speech engine Bolikh uses isn’t available for Intel Macs at the moment.

macOS won’t open Bolikh (“Apple could not verify…”)

Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll down, click Open Anyway next to the Bolikh message, and confirm. You only need to do this the first time.

macOS says Bolikh “is damaged and can’t be opened”

The download is fine. macOS shows this for apps that aren’t notarized by Apple yet. Move Bolikh to Applications, open Terminal and run:

xattr -cr /Applications/Bolikh.app

Then open Bolikh again.

I switched on Accessibility, but Bolikh keeps asking

macOS remembers the permission for one exact build of Bolikh, so after installing or updating it can show Bolikh as switched on while still treating the new version as not allowed. Switching it off and on isn’t enough; remove it and add it again:

  1. Quit Bolikh (menu bar icon → Quit Bolikh).
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  3. Select every Bolikh entry (and any old Aazad Voice entry) and remove it with the − button.
  4. Open Bolikh again, click Open Settings in its setup screen and switch on the new Bolikh entry.

If the list won’t let you remove it, run this in Terminal, then do step 4:

tccutil reset Accessibility com.aazad.voice
