MacWhisper alternative · free

A free MacWhisper alternative for live dictation.

MacWhisper is best known for transcribing recordings: you give it an audio or video file and get the text. Bolikh is built for the other job: you speak now, and the words are typed where your cursor is. If live dictation is what you are after, Bolikh is free and also runs on Windows and Linux.

Choose Bolikh if

  • You want to type by voice in any app, all day, with one key.
  • You also use Windows or Linux and want the same app everywhere.
  • You don’t want to pay for dictation.

Choose MacWhisper if

  • You need to transcribe audio or video files, podcasts or meetings. Bolikh doesn’t do that.
  • You want subtitles or other export formats from a recording.
  • You want to pick between Whisper and Parakeet models.

Questions

Can Bolikh transcribe an audio file like MacWhisper?

No. Bolikh only types what you say into the microphone. For files, MacWhisper is the right tool.

Is Bolikh free on Mac?

Yes. It is free and open source, and needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer).

Do both work offline?

Yes. Both turn speech into text on your Mac.
