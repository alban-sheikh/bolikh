Bolikh / Privacy

Privacy policy · updated 12 November 2026

What Bolikh does with your data.

In short: your voice is turned into text on your own computer, and nothing about your dictation is sent to us.

Audio

When you hold the dictation key, Bolikh records from your microphone into memory. As soon as you let go, the audio is turned into text on your computer and then discarded. Audio is never written to disk and never uploaded.

Text and history

The text is typed into the app you are using. If History is on (the default), your last 200 dictations are saved in a file in Bolikh's settings folder on your computer. You can clear it or turn it off in the app. Your dictionary and settings are stored the same way.

Clipboard

To type the text, Bolikh briefly places it on your clipboard, pastes it, and then restores what was there before. It does not keep or send clipboard contents.

Internet connections

Bolikh connects to the internet for one thing: downloading the speech model (NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3, about 670 MB) from Hugging Face on first launch, or again if you choose Retry. Hugging Face sees a normal download request from your IP address. After that, Bolikh works offline.

Accounts, analytics and tracking

There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising in the app. This website does not use cookies. Bolikh’s source code is public, so you can check all of this yourself.

Permissions

Microphone, to hear you. On macOS, Accessibility, to notice the dictation key in other apps and to paste. On Linux, access to input devices for the key. These are used only for dictation.

If you contact us

If you email or message us, we use your details only to reply and to send you the download you asked for. We do not sell or share them.
