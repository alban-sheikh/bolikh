Superwhisper alternative · free

A free Superwhisper alternative, with Linux included.

Superwhisper and Bolikh can both turn speech into text on your own device. Superwhisper gives you many models and AI modes, and keeps the more advanced ones for its paid plan. Bolikh does less on purpose: one good speech model, text clean-up built in, everything free.

Choose Bolikh if

  • You want the Parakeet speech model without paying. In Superwhisper it is part of Pro; in Bolikh it is the only model and it is free.
  • You use Linux, which Superwhisper doesn’t support.
  • You want something with nothing to configure: install, hold a key, speak.
  • You prefer open-source software.

Choose Superwhisper if

  • You want dictation on your phone as well.
  • You want AI modes that turn rough speech into an email, a summary or a different tone.
  • You want to choose between several speech models, including cloud ones.
  • You speak a language outside Bolikh’s 25. Whisper models cover more.

Questions

Is there a free alternative to Superwhisper?

Yes. Bolikh is completely free and open source. Superwhisper also has a free plan with local Whisper models; its Parakeet model, cloud models and AI modes need Pro.

Does Bolikh run on Linux?

Yes. Bolikh has installers for Ubuntu, Debian and Fedora, and works on X11 and Wayland.

What will I miss if I switch from Superwhisper to Bolikh?

Phone apps, AI modes, a choice of models and meeting recording. Bolikh only does live dictation.
